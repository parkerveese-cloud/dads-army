Dads Army shows this page in the app, under Help → Credits.

Thank you.

Dads Army is made by people who give their time, skills and ideas freely. They share one hope: that whatever comes, whether a flood, a storm, days without power or water, or wider disruption to everyday life, households across the UK are ready, calm and able to look after one another.

Every line of code, idea, test and word of feedback here has helped a household somewhere be a little better prepared. To everyone named below: thank you.
